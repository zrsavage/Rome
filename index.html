LEGIO

From the Gallic frontier to the gates of Rome

v0.2 - Act I: six battles, campaign map, camp phase.

Controls
Left click / drag: select · Right click: move or attack
Right drag: draw a battle line · Wheel: zoom · WASD / arrows: pan
Space pause · Z X C speed 1x/2x/4x · Q select all
Ctrl+1-9 set control group · 1-9 recall (double-tap: centre camera)
H hold & stop · T testudo / loose order · V range rings · Esc deselect
Arcs under a unit: green front, yellow flanks, red rear.
You can give orders while paused. Hit enemy flanks and rears; rout breaks armies.
