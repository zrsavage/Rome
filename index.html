From the Gallic frontier to the gates of Rome

Command a legion of Caesar from the fords of the Rhone in 58 BC to the last battle of the civil war at Munda: six acts and a side act in Britain, thirty-four battles.

Your campaigns

Music, sources and settings: the speaker button, bottom left.

Controls
Left click / drag: select · Ctrl+click: add or remove a unit · Shift+click a unit card: select every card between it and the last one clicked (Ctrl+Shift adds them) · Right click: move or attack
Right drag: draw a battle line (it faces left of the way you draw) · Wheel: zoom · WASD / arrows: pan
Space pause · Z X C speed 1x/2x/4x · - / = slower / faster (down to 0.5x) · Q select all
Ctrl + right-click marches without stopping to fight
Ctrl+1-9 set control group · 1-9 recall (double-tap: centre camera)
E select your general (again to centre); units in his gold ring rally faster
· V range rings · Esc deselect
Arcs under a unit: green front, yellow flanks, red rear.
You can give orders while paused. Hit enemy flanks and rears; rout breaks armies.
